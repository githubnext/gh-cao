# Phase ownership and specialist use

The [actual roster](team.md) contains 85 specialists and four support members.
Use the actual charter for technical detail, not this ownership index as a
generic replacement. [Routing](routing.md) retains all 85 specialist IDs.

- **Assessor:** static observed facts, dependency inventory and evidence gaps,
  not recommendations or source implementation.
- **Product Analyst:** confirmed vs assumed requirements, stakeholders,
  preservation intent, traceability and human decision/acceptance ownership.
- **Classifier:** six-R comparisons, compatibility and sourced cost assumptions;
  not customer selection or implementation.
- **Planner:** central integrated plan, cross-repository contracts, dependency
  DAG, per-repository issue scope, measurable exits and data/rollback integration.
  Planning does not authorize or automatically execute its backlog.
- **Execution Orchestrator:** bounded specialist sequencing inside the single
  CAO-admitted target. Not fleet discovery, cross-repository dispatch or a
  replacement for independent review, validation or human authorization.
- **Domain specialists:** their real database/runtime/framework/platform/client
  expertise applies to assessment, selected-path planning, scoped implementation
  and validation evidence. Preserve distinct semantics and qualified ownership.
- **Reviewer:** one independent consolidated revision-bound artifact/diff verdict,
  with bounded independent domain input as needed; never an author of that work.
- **Validator:** acceptance-design consultation plus independently measured
  outcomes and cross-repository contract verification within admitted scope.
  Proposed tests and authors' self-checks are not independent outcome proof.
- **Documentation:** evidence-linked runbooks, playbooks, technical writing and
  handover, not ownership of domain or customer decisions.
- **Scribe:** accepted decision merging through available permitted state tools;
  not session history synthesis, technical decisions or Git publication.
- **Ralph:** explicitly requested bounded status reports on supplied work only.
  No automatic discovery, queue polling, dispatch or keep-working loops.
- **Rai:** requested Responsible AI consultation or selected bounded gate;
  not a substitute for Security/Privacy, independent review or validation.
- **Fact Checker:** requested verification/counter-hypothesis evidence or selected
  bounded gate; not automatic review fan-out or self-certification.

Business acceptance, legal/compliance decisions, risk waivers and deployment
destination selection belong to authorized humans. Technical capability, a
support identity, or a routing row grants no operational permissions.
