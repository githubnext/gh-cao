# Required handoff shapes

Use these lean shapes with the full [contracts](contracts.md); link immutable
artifacts rather than duplicating sensitive records. They are output contracts,
not executable workflow or framework scaffolding templates.

## Assessment

- Target and source revision/diff digest; inspected paths and method.
- Observed stack, dependencies, contracts, consumers and data/write owners.
- Facts with citations; assumptions, unknowns, exclusions and decision impact.
- Existing executed evidence distinguished from static observations.

## Central proposal and approved issue

- Proposal ID/revision; confirmed requirements and human option/destination
  selection; actual independent review and human approval records.
- Cross-repository contract versions and dependency DAG with accountable owners.
- Per-target approved paths/actions/commands, explicit mode and environments.
- Predecessor artifact revisions, required proof and satisfaction criteria.
- Acceptance method/threshold owner, independent reviewer/verifier, stop criteria.
- Data/coexistence/rollback plan and separately scoped operational decisions.
- Approved issue identity and output `draft-pull-request`; no auto merge.

## Implementation handoff

- Admission envelope, approved issue/plan/source revisions and dependency proof.
- Actual changed paths and exact diff digest/revision; accountable specialist.
- Each permitted command, tool/environment, actual result and retained proof.
- Regression/negative/characterization cases and remaining evidence gaps.
- Independent review verdict on that diff and independent validation findings.
- Draft-PR safe-output payload, without direct commit/push/publication.

## Review finding

- Exact artifact/diff revision and qualified independent reviewer identity.
- Verdict `pass`, `fail` or `blocked`; requirement and concrete evidence.
- Severity, affected scope, minimum correction/approval condition and next owner.
- Revision-bound rereview disposition; no self-approval or operational grant.

## Cross-repository verification

- Approved contract/dependency criteria and exact participating revisions.
- Independent verifier; authorized method, environment, actual observations.
- Criterion-by-criterion `pass`, `fail`, `blocked` or `not-attempted`, with proof.
- Missing targets/commands/evidence, impact, owner and closure condition.
- Human handover; merge/deployment/production authority remains separate.
