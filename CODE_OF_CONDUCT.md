# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to an open, welcoming, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, appearance, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on ideas and outcomes, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
language or attention, trolling, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and
publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in
public or private community interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
<opensource@github.com>. Do not include sensitive information beyond what is
needed to understand the report.

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
appropriate action based on the context and severity of a violation. Reports
will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
